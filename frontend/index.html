NeoMortalSurv

Research calculator that estimates the probability of survival of very-low-birth-weight (VLBW) infants treated in the neonatal intensive care unit, at days 7, 28 and 100, with a published DeepSurv model. Available in English, Russian and Kazakh.

Bozhbanbayeva N, Abilbayeva A, Tarabayeva A, Isgandarov I, Tolykbayev T, Adilbekova I, Yelyubayeva D. A deep-learning approach to predict mortality in very-low-birth-weight infants treated in the intensive care units using DeepSurv. Front. Pediatr. 2026;14:1801840. doi:10.3389/fped.2026.1801840

For research use. Not a substitute for clinical judgement.
